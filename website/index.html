From a message
to a payment.

The Android overlay picks up the details. You stay in control of the send.

  1. 01

    Tap to capture

    Beam reads accessible text when you tap the overlay. Paste the details if the app can’t expose its text.

  2. 02

    Review who and how much

    Check the amount and recipient. Beam resolves ENS names and rechecks the address on your phone.

  3. 03

    Confirm on your phone

    Beam checks the transaction before signing. Confirm on your phone to send test USDC through BeamPay on Arc Testnet.

Some requests need a second approval.

When a payment exceeds your cap or comes from an external agent, Beam asks you to approve the request in World App with your linked World ID. You still confirm the payment on your phone.

Try the World ID flow ↑
